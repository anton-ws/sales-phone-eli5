Part 2How the record works

Think of it as a logbook next to the phone. After every call, someone writes down who picked up and puts a mark next to that number.

Here is what happens every 20 minutes, while the US team is calling:

flowchart TD
  A["Take the new calls from Trellus,
with their transcripts"] --> B["Read each call:
who answered?"]
  B --> C["Give the number a grade:
proven / own voicemail / machine / dead / not tested"]
  C --> D["Look at the Apollo card
and the Salesforce phone history:
did the number change, and who changed it?"]
  D --> E["Update the fix list
and the morning report"]
Why it is safe against the sync problem
  • It keeps its own copy. No sync, Apollo rule or rep can overwrite it.
  • It only reads. Right now it cannot make anything worse.
  • A grade needs a real recording as proof, which is the rule we agreed on 22 Sep.
  • Every day it reports each phone change in Salesforce: who made it, and whether the next call on the new number reached a person. This is the "who overwrote it" report Greg asked for.

How we checked that it reads calls correctly

We compared it with 3,087 calls that were read one by one: it agrees on 91% of them. Then we checked random samples by hand: 97 of 100 "menu or recording" calls were right, and 49 of 50 "voicemail" calls. About 380 calls the rules could not place were read one by one.

Part 3What the record has shown us so far

1. Two Apollo workflows rewrite our phone numbers

"Phone enrichment if added to the sequence": when a rep adds a contact to any sequence, Apollo buys a phone number again and writes it into the Salesforce Phone field about 3 minutes later. 13,146 runs, 45,219 credits so far.

"Enrich new number {bad phone number stage}": when a contact is moved to "Bad Phone Number", Apollo buys another number. 10,595 runs, 30,341 credits so far.

509 phone numbers in Salesforce were rewritten this way in the last 30 days. It usually happens before the first call. Neither workflow checks that the new number reaches a person. Salesforce shows these changes under Anton's name, because Anton's login connects Apollo to Salesforce.

2. Together they make a loop

A working number gets replaced by a bought one. The bought one doesn't answer. The dialer logs "Bad / Wrong Number", the contact goes to "Bad Phone Number", and Apollo buys yet another number.

161 contacts are in "Bad Phone Number" now. For 43 of them, a number on the card has already reached them. Only 22 really have nothing that works.

3. The company main lines are the biggest waste

39% of all calls go to a company main line that Apollo bought. On those, we reached the person 18 times in 1,855 calls. By rep: Denise 47% of her calls, Jonathan 36%, Winston 26%.

4. We still call numbers we already know are machines

8% of calls this month went to a number that had already answered with a machine. The morning report counts this every day, per rep. The goal is zero.

Two corrections to what we said on 22 Sep
  • The overwrites are not the Salesforce sync. It is the two Apollo workflows above.
  • Winston does not mark bad numbers by hand. His dialer account marks every unanswered call as "Bad / Wrong Number" automatically, including calls our own dialer hung up. So those marks are not a reliable signal.

Part 4Fixes: applied and ready

Applied in Apollo, Trellus or Salesforce: none. That was the agreement. On our side, the record is built and running. The four fixes below are ready to switch on.

FIX 1 · ONE SWITCH PER REP

Stop calling Apollo's company main lines

What changes
Trellus: "Corporate Phone" off for Denise, Jonathan and Winston. Nick keeps it.
Effect
About 1,600 fewer wasted calls a month (36% of their calls). We lose about 5 of 49 good outcomes. 1,168 of the 1,532 contacts affected still have another number.
How we'll know
The next morning's report.
FIX 2 · 65 CONTACTS

Put the proven number first

What changes
For 65 contacts, the number that already led to a conversation becomes the default in Apollo.
Why it works
The dialer calls the default number first in 68% of cases.
How we'll know
The record checks their next calls.
FIX 3 · NEEDS THE WORKFLOW OWNER

Stop Apollo replacing a number that works

What changes
For example: skip contacts whose number is proven, or stop the workflows from writing into the Salesforce Phone field.
Effect
Ends the loop and saves credits.
How we'll know
The daily "who changed the phone" report goes quiet.
FIX 4 · ONE SETTING

Log phone menus as "phone menu"

What changes
Trellus, "What to Log": give "Phone Menu" its own outcome. Today it is logged as "No Answer".
Effect
The CRM itself shows which numbers are machines.
How we'll know
Menu outcomes appear in Salesforce.
